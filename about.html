THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Hi, I’m Antonio.

I’m a UX/UI Designer with a background in fashion and visual arts. My path began with a curiosity for visual expression and evolved into an interest in how people interact with digital products.

After studying painting at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Napoli, I continued my training in UX/UI Design with Start2impact University, exploring user research, accessibility, wireframing, prototyping and interface design.

I believe good design should feel intuitive, communicate clearly and make technology more human.
